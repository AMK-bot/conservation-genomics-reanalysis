Can we trust a conservation-genomics verdict?

A two-page research brief

Project type: Reproducible computational reanalysis of two public whole-genome datasets

Species: Green peafowl (Pavo muticus) and Scandinavian wolf (Canis lupus)

Central finding: The peafowl’s categorical result is stable even when its estimates are fragile; the wolf’s load magnitude changes with thresholds while the realized-versus-masked pattern remains stable.

Page 1 of 2 — Question, design, and results

The biological question

Conservation indicators compress complex genomic evidence into a decision. This project asks:

Does the decision remain the same when an analyst changes reasonable filtering, population-structure, deleterious-site, or run-of-homozygosity choices?

The goal is not to find the single most dramatic number. It is to separate robust conclusions from fragile magnitudes and insufficient evidence.

Three results to remember

1. A robust peafowl headline

Across all three filter presets, 0 of 3 sampled populations confidently pass Ne > 500. Vietnam’s lenient point estimate is above 500, but its 90% interval (139–37,795) crosses the threshold, so the result is indeterminate rather than a pass. Phase 2 evidence

2. Fragile estimates beneath that headline

Two of three populations change between fail and indeterminate as filtering changes, and several stricter-filter estimates do not converge. The correct conclusion is therefore not “all three Ne values are known to be below 500.” It is “none confidently clears 500 with these data and tested choices.” Phase 3 evidence

3. A stable wolf pattern with threshold-sensitive magnitude

At GERP  > 4, the workflow reproduces the deposit’s realized and masked loads across all 211 wolves to floating-point precision, with zero raw-count differences. Mean realized load changes from 0.172 to 0.136 to 0.093 as the threshold rises from 2 to 4, and mean FROH changes from 0.226 to 0.167 as the ROH cutoff rises from 0.1 to 2.0 Mb. The absolute values move, while the inbred-Scandinavian realized-versus-masked direction remains stable. Phase 4 evidence

Why the result matters

The project demonstrates two forms of scientific maturity:

  • A stable category does not make every underlying estimate precise.
  • An uncertain or non-converged estimate is information about the limits of the data, not an invitation to report the point estimate alone.

The project’s country-scale Populations Maintained calculation is 0.70, with a 0.70–0.70 band after the live BirdLife country table was checked on 18 July 2026. All three sampled countries are counted among the maintained cases, while none of their sampled populations confidently clears Ne > 500. This illustrates the difference between continued occurrence and demonstrated long-term genetic security. The occurrence table remains coarse at country scale and needs finer occurrence evidence for publication-level use. GBF evidence

Simply adding SNPs is not the first remedy for the peafowl result: currentNe2 already samples up to 25,000 SNPs per cell, while the number of individuals remains 7/10/5.

Claim boundary

This reanalysis identifies conservation risk and analytical sensitivity, not demonstrated inbreeding depression. The temporal peafowl comparison is a weak contrast, not an erosion claim. GONE2 is context rather than part of the contemporary verdict. The wolf anchor proves computational fidelity to the deposited calculation, not that every deleteriousness score predicts fitness.

Reproducibility evidence

The four tracked phase validators report 32 PASS, 12 reported-not-asserted WARN, and 0 FAIL. Warnings surface scientific sensitivity rather than hiding it:

  • Phase 1 — QC, structure, diversity, and temporal contrast.
  • Phase 2 — currentNe2 cube, convergence, and context panels.
  • Phase 3 — GBF tables reconciled to the cube.
  • Phase 4 — exact wolf anchor and threshold sweeps.

Contribution and assistance boundary

The source studies supplied the biological samples, deposited variants, annotations, and original methods. The student project contributes the cross-cutting fragility question, the filter-by-structure cube, the three-state verdict, the separation of contemporary and trajectory estimates, the wolf sensitivity extensions, and the synthesis of what is robust versus unresolved. Existing bioinformatics software performs the calculations. AI coding assistance supported planning, implementation, debugging, review, and drafting; the student remains responsible for the chosen decisions, validation checks, and scientific claims.
